Before you go anywhere
Every visitor must follow these requirements before entering any Chislett Farms site area.
🧑
Stay with your Chislett Farms host at all timesYou must not move around the site unaccompanied. Your host is responsible for your safety. Do not wander into areas you have not been taken to.
🦺
Wear hi-vis at all times on siteA hi-vis vest must be worn as soon as you enter the site and kept on until you leave. Hi-vis vests are available at the site entrance. Ask your host if you need one.
👟
Wear closed-toe shoesThongs, sandals, and open-toe footwear are not permitted on any Chislett Farms site. If you are not wearing appropriate footwear you may be asked to wait at reception.
📋

Scene 3 of 7
Rules while you are on site
These rules apply to all visitors for the duration of your time on any Chislett Farms property.
  • Follow all directions from your Chislett Farms host without exception.
  • Stay on the paths and in areas your host has taken you to. Do not explore independently.
  • Maximum vehicle speed on all sites is 10 km/h or as signed. Give way to pedestrians and farm machinery at all times.
  • Park only in designated visitor parking areas as directed by your host.
  • Do not use your mobile phone while walking near machinery, vehicles, or active work areas.
  • Do not touch, operate, or interfere with any equipment, machinery, or produce unless specifically requested and supervised by your host.
  • Photography and video recording may only be conducted with prior permission from Chislett Farms management.
  • No alcohol, illegal drugs, or weapons are permitted on any Chislett Farms site at any time.
  • NO GLASS OR CONTAMINANTS of any kind are permitted in the Packing Shed, Nursery, or on any farm site. This is a strict food safety requirement. This includes glass drink bottles, broken items, or any foreign objects.
  • Do not enter cold storage, chemical storage, or any locked or restricted area without explicit permission and supervision.

Scene 5 of 7
In an emergency
Please know what to do if something goes wrong during your visit.
EMERGENCY NUMBER
000
Fire, Ambulance, Police
  • 1Stay calm and stay with your host.
  • 2Follow your host's instructions immediately and without hesitation.
  • 3If instructed to evacuate, proceed directly to the emergency assembly point.
  • 4Do not return to collect belongings during an evacuation.
  • 5If someone is injured, call 000 and notify your host immediately.
  • 6Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate further danger.
  • 7Remain at the assembly point until given the all-clear by site management.
Your host will point out the emergency assembly point at the start of your visit. If they have not done this, please ask before entering the site.
Confidential safety concerns can be raised with the HR and Quality Assurance Manager: Gretchen at gretchen@chislettfarms.com.au
